About the data
  • Course info comes from sfucourses.com. Each course's prerequisites are re-checked against SFU's official outline for its latest offering.
  • * marks courses whose requirements were read from SFU's wording by this tool (because sfucourses was out of date or had none). Those are best-effort.
  • Only course requirements are drawn. Unit counts (“60 units”), minimum grades, high-school courses and program-specific conditions are left out, so “ready” only means you have the course prereqs.
  • Corequisites aren't drawn either; they're listed in the hover and details for the few courses that have them.
  • “Retired” means not offered in about 2 years. Offering history only goes back to Spring 2024.
